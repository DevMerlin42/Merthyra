Overview

Welcome to Merthyra

Merthyra is an Apple-native workspace for writing, planning, organizing, and understanding your work in one place. It combines a professional Writer with projects, tasks, calendar, spreadsheets, files, people, automations, analytics, integrations, and workspace-aware intelligence.

Current documentation: This guide reflects Build 63, the App Store submission release with the unified Writer and native Mac, iPhone, iPad, and visionOS destinations.

What makes Merthyra different

One connected system

Writer pages, tasks, projects, events, files, people, and automations can be searched and understood together.

File-backed by design

Core records are saved as readable app files in Merthyra’s workspace storage rather than an invented remote database.

Workspace-scoped

Every major record belongs to an active workspace, preventing one workspace from leaking into another.

Apple-native

Merthyra uses SwiftUI-first interfaces and integrates with iCloud, Calendar, Maps, Spotlight, Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, Live Activities, and more where supported.

Main destinations

The main app includes Home, Assistant, Writer, Projects, Spreadsheets, Calendar, Tasks, Files, People, Analytics, Automations, and Integrations. Mac and iPad use a spacious sidebar. iPhone keeps Home, Writer, Tasks, Calendar, and More in the bottom tab bar, with the remaining destinations inside More.

Start here

Getting started

1. Sign in and choose a workspace

Merthyra supports Sign in with Apple and creates or restores Personal Workspace. Custom workspaces appear in the sidebar or iPhone More view. The active workspace controls which pages, tasks, projects, files, people, automations, and dashboard layouts are visible.

2. Customize Home

Open Customize Home to enable, disable, and reorder dashboard panels. Available panels include Today, Quick Actions, Projects, Work Queue, Creator Flow, Writer, Automations, Team Activity, Analytics Overview, Knowledge Graph, Connected Account Analytics, the Lloer Assistant card, and Quick Capture.

3. Create your first page

Open Writer and choose New Page. You can begin with a note, article, writing project, book, chapter, scene, Daily Note, or Scratch Pad. Pages save automatically and remain inside the current workspace.

4. Add work around the page

Create tasks, project records, calendar events, spreadsheets, or files as the work grows. Use global search or Merthyra Intelligence to retrieve context across these areas later.

5. Keep backups

Although Merthyra includes automatic saving, iCloud synchronization, conflict merging, Trash, version history, and workspace export, beta users should still keep independent backups of important work.

Structure

Navigation & workspaces

Mac and iPad

The primary sidebar groups destinations into Main, General, Operate, and Workspaces. Writer appears first in General, followed by Projects, Spreadsheets, Calendar, Tasks, and Files. People, Analytics, Automations, and Integrations live in Operate.

iPhone

The bottom bar is ordered Home, Writer, Tasks, Calendar, and More. More contains Spreadsheets, Projects, Files, People, Analytics, Automations, Integrations, workspaces, and Settings. Writer uses a drill-in flow: library first, then a full-width editor.

Workspace isolation

Each active workspace has its own Writer pages and sections, suite records, dashboard layout, integration preferences, collapse state, and search scope. Switching workspaces reloads the relevant stores instead of filtering a global fake database.

Workspace invitations

People can create a readable invitation file for an Apple email address. Merthyra opens the device Mail composer with a secure deep link and never sends the email without review. Membership and invitation status are represented as file-backed records.

Deep links and system routes

Merthyra routes supported links directly to Writer, projects, tasks, files, calendar, integrations, focus sessions, Home, Assistant, and workspace invitations. Legacy Writer and Wiki routes redirect to the unified Writer.

Dashboard

Home

Home is a configurable overview of the active workspace rather than a fixed marketing dashboard.

Dashboard panels

  • Today: current tasks and calendar events.
  • Quick Actions: create notes, projects, tasks, events, dashboards, and other records.
  • Projects: filter All, Active, Planning, On Hold, or Completed projects.
  • Work Queue: active tasks and status.
  • Creator Flow: production stages and items.
  • Writer: recent or important pages.
  • Automations: reusable automation templates and rules.
  • Team Activity: recent workspace actions.
  • Analytics Overview: completion and workspace metrics.
  • Knowledge Graph: visual relationships in the workspace.
  • Connected Account Analytics: supported creator metrics when a provider is connected.
  • Lloer Assistant Card: workspace-aware prompts.
  • Quick Capture: fast entry into the workspace.

Customize Home

Use the dashboard editor to toggle panels and move them earlier or later. Quick Actions remains pinned so the dashboard always has a reliable creation surface. Layout preferences are saved per workspace and synchronized with the rest of the workspace data.

Create

Writer

Writer is Merthyra’s only writing system. The legacy Writer and Wiki were migrated into this unified, file-backed model.

Pages and document types

Every page has a stable ID, workspace, title, summary, body, section, symbol, tags, syntax-coloring preference, pin and favorite state, archive state, document type, optional parent, icon color, word goal, typography settings, writing-aid settings, attachment search text, and creation or modification dates.

Document types include Note, Article, Project, Book, Chapter, Scene, Section, Daily Note, Scratch Pad, and compatibility Template records. Long-form pages can be nested and reordered.

Sections

Sections can be created, renamed, reordered, collapsed, expanded, and deleted. Collapse state is remembered by workspace ID and section ID. Deleting a populated section lets you keep its pages by moving them safely or delete the contained pages as well.

Tags

Nested tags such as #project/merthyra/design remain editable until you type terminating whitespace. Only then is the token removed from the body and added to metadata. Duplicates are normalized case-insensitively. Clicking a tag pill returns editable tag text at the current cursor.

Tabs and dual documents

Open pages remain in stable tab positions; selecting a tab highlights it without moving it to the end. On supported larger layouts, right-click another tab and choose Open Dual Tabs to show the original page on the left and the selected page on the right while closing the sidebars.

Trash and archive

Deleted pages and sections move into workspace-scoped Writer Trash. You can restore them, delete individual items forever, or empty Trash. Tombstones and purge records prevent stale iCloud conflict copies from returning permanently deleted records.

Editor

Markdown & preview

Editor layouts

  • Editor: raw Markdown editing.
  • Preview: rendered reading view.
  • Split: editor beside a live preview.
  • Combined: the inline live-rendered Writer experience.

Layout selection lives in Page Settings so the main toolbar stays minimal. iPhone adapts side-by-side content into a compact vertical or drill-in presentation.

Supported syntax

Writer handles common Markdown headings, emphasis, links, quotes, lists, dividers, task lists, tables, footnotes, fenced code blocks, image references, attachments, LaTeX-style math blocks, Mermaid flowcharts, callouts, and image galleries. Rendering is broad but is not represented as complete certification against every CommonMark or GFM conformance case.

Syntax coloring

Each page can enable or disable syntax coloring. The combined editor styles Markdown structure and writing analysis while preserving selection, cursor position, and scroll position on iPhone. Code fences and intentional Markdown line breaks are excluded from inappropriate lint warnings.

Linting

The Markdown linter reports informational, warning, and error-level issues such as unclosed fences, malformed links, and structural problems. Lint results are intended to explain a document issue, not replace the editor body or force changes.

Focus

Writing tools & insights

Zen Mode

Zen Mode closes both left sidebars on desktop and hides the iPhone bottom tab bar. The current page remains full-width while its word-goal progress line stays visible at the bottom. Exit Zen restores the prior navigation state.

Typewriter Mode

Typewriter Mode keeps the active cursor line near the vertical center of the editor while typing. It works with preserved selection and viewport restoration so syntax styling does not jump long documents to the top.

Writing aids

Each page can independently toggle parts-of-speech highlighting, passive voice, weak verbs, adverbs, filler words, clichés, repeated words, unnecessary words, and long sentences. Native spelling and grammar tools remain available where supported by the platform.

Document insights

Page Info combines word, character, sentence, and paragraph counts with reading time, speaking time, page estimate, readability score, grade level, average sentence length, paragraph density, dialogue percentage, and vocabulary diversity.

Goals and sessions

Set a page word target and track progress in normal or Zen layouts. Writer records supported writing sessions and can restore the last session, selected page, open tabs, cursor location, and scroll position after restart.

Version history

Writer saves version snapshots and exposes history from the page context menu. Restoring a version updates the current page while preserving the page’s stable identity and workspace ownership.

Knowledge

Links, media & export

Wiki links and backlinks

Use [[Page Title]] or an alias to connect pages. Writer can show outgoing links, backlinks, suggested links, broken links, connected notes, and hover previews on supported pointer platforms.

Media and attachments

Writer supports images, audio, video, PDFs, and general attachments. Drag-and-drop importing is available on supported platforms. Media is copied into Merthyra’s managed storage rather than depending on a temporary picker URL.

OCR

Supported images and PDFs can contribute OCR text to page and workspace search. OCR output is stored as searchable attachment context and does not silently rewrite the visible page body.

Handwriting

iPhone and iPad can show a PencilKit handwriting sheet. Apple Pencil is supported on compatible iPad models, handwriting can be converted to text, and a drawing can be inserted into a typed page. Handwriting controls are hidden on Mac.

Image tools

Supported images can be copied non-destructively for crop, resize, rotation, and system-supported foreground or background removal. The source attachment remains available.

Export and backup

Writer can export Markdown, PDF, DOCX, HTML, ePub, RTF, and plain text. It also includes a full Writer workspace backup path. Export fidelity depends on the destination format; complex interactive content may be flattened.

Data

Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets provides modern workbooks without copying the old desktop spreadsheet formula-bar-first experience into every device.

Workbooks and sheets

Create blank workbooks or choose a useful template, then add, rename, duplicate, and delete sheets. Workbooks and sheets are workspace-scoped and save in Merthyra’s iCloud Drive storage.

Editing

Click or tap a cell and type directly into it. The selected cell remains visibly highlighted. A formula field is available for values and expressions such as =SUM(A1:A8).

Formulas

The formula engine supports cell references, ranges, arithmetic, and functions including SUM, AVERAGE or AVG, MIN, MAX, and COUNT. Invalid formulas return a readable cell error rather than crashing the workbook.

Formatting and size

Apply bold, italic, alignment, number formats, and supported cell appearance settings. Expand a sheet by adding rows or columns as the workbook grows.

CSV

Import a CSV file into a workbook and export the current sheet as CSV. CSV preserves tabular values but does not preserve Merthyra-specific formatting or formulas in every external spreadsheet app.

Plan

Projects & Creator Flow

Projects

Create projects with a title, short goal, status, dates, notes, tasks, and supporting information. Home can filter projects by All, Active, Planning, On Hold, or Completed. Project counts and completion feed workspace analytics.

Creator Flow

Creator Flow is a customizable production board for videos, posts, campaigns, releases, and client work. Stages can be renamed, recolored, moved earlier or later, or deleted. Items can hold a title, notes, due date, assigned person, and workflow stage.

Responsive board behavior

Stages wrap across the available window and grow vertically. The page uses the normal scroll wheel or touch scrolling rather than forcing a permanently horizontal kanban strip.

Workspace relationship

Project and Creator Flow records save as real workspace records. They can appear on Home, contribute to analytics, and be indexed for global search and Merthyra Intelligence.

Execute

Tasks

Task fields

Tasks include a title, notes, workspace or space, status, priority, due date, estimate, assignment, tags, and completion information. Status values include Inbox, Planned, In Progress, Review, Blocked, and Done. Priority values include None, Low, Medium, High, and Urgent.

Work Queue

Home’s Work Queue and the dedicated Tasks destination surface active work. Filters and workspace switching ensure tasks are shown only in the correct context.

Notifications and dates

Supported due dates can schedule local notifications after permission is granted. System delivery is controlled by device settings.

System entry points

Create tasks through the app, Quick Actions, Siri, Shortcuts, Action Button assignments, widgets, or Merthyra Intelligence actions where supported.

Time

Calendar

Events and calendars

Create events with title, notes, start and end time, all-day status, recurrence, alerts, time zones, URLs, attendees, and calendar ownership. Events are scoped to the active workspace.

Apple Calendar import

After EventKit permission is granted, choose available Apple calendars to import. Imported event information is copied into Merthyra. The import path is designed not to modify or delete the source Apple Calendar event.

Maps and locations

Search Apple Maps, use current location after permission, or place a custom pin. Saved locations can include a display name, editable address, coordinates, Apple Place ID, time-zone identifier, and custom-pin state.

Conflict handling

Calendar files use stable record IDs and modification dates. Deletion records help prevent older iCloud conflict versions from restoring removed events.

App Intents

Merthyra includes intents to create a calendar event, check the calendar, and retrieve the next event, alongside deep links that open Calendar directly.

Library

Files & assets

Workspace Files

Drag files onto the Files page or use Import. iPhone and iPad can also show Scan Document through VisionKit. Merthyra categorizes files, shows size and modification date, and stores them in its iCloud Drive Files folder.

Preview and editing

Images and PDFs use native previews. Supported text files can open in the built-in text editor. Other file types can be organized and opened in the appropriate system app.

Assets

Asset records can represent images, video, audio, documents, design files, archives, links, or other material. They can be attached to a workspace or project context and indexed for search.

Deletion

Deleting a file removes it from Merthyra’s managed Files folder. Confirm destructive actions and maintain independent backups for important source material.

Collaborate

People & workspaces

People records

People stores workspace member or invitation information, display name, Apple email, role, notes, and membership status. Records remain scoped to the workspace.

Invitation flow

Enter the Apple email connected to the recipient’s Merthyra account, optionally add a display name, and choose a workspace. Merthyra creates a readable invitation file and opens Mail with the invitation deep link. You review the email before sending.

Status

Membership states include Invite Pending and Workspace Member. Invitations can be resent or removed. Merthyra does not silently claim that a person joined before the invitation is accepted.

Reuse

Automations & templates

Writing templates

From a Writer page, choose Save as Automation Template and assign a keyword. Merthyra stores the page body inside the Automations Templates folder. Typing the keyword followed by whitespace inside any Writer page inserts the stored content at the cursor.

Variables

Templates can use supported fields, dates, counters, snippets, and a cursor placeholder to determine where editing continues after expansion.

Automation rules

Rules can respond to Task Created, Task Completed, Due Date Approaching, Project Status Changed, Document Created, or Scheduled Time. Actions include Move Task, Add Tag, Create Task, Create Document, Update Project, and Show Reminder.

Transparency

Automation records are file-backed and visible. Merthyra does not pretend a rule ran if its trigger or action is unavailable on the current device.

Understand

Analytics

Analytics summarizes records already inside the active workspace rather than inventing sample data.

Workspace metrics

Project counts, completion percentage, people, tasks, Writer pages, recent activity, and Creator Flow records can contribute to the overview.

Home analytics

Analytics Overview and Connected Account Analytics can be enabled on Home. Connected account cards appear only for providers with a verified connection and dashboard visibility enabled.

Creator accounts

Supported providers include Twitch, YouTube, Instagram, Bluesky, and LinkedIn. Metrics depend on provider authorization, available APIs, configured OAuth credentials, and the permissions granted by the user.

Integrations

Apple services

  • Apple Calendar
  • Spotlight
  • iCloud Drive
  • Siri & Shortcuts
  • Sign in with Apple

Creator accounts

Supported connection types include Twitch, YouTube, Instagram, Bluesky, and LinkedIn. Integration cards open from anywhere on the card, not only the label text.

Secure browser sign-in

Configured providers use a secure browser authorization flow and callback validation. Merthyra never asks for the provider password inside the app. A provider cannot be marked connected until the authorization callback and token exchange succeed.

Development configuration

OAuth providers require registered client IDs, callback URLs, and Merthyra’s secure token-exchange configuration. A local development build without those values can open the official provider website but cannot complete account linking.

AI

Merthyra Intelligence

Merthyra retrieves relevant workspace context and uses Apple Foundation Models on supported devices to help find, explain, draft, and organize work.

Workspace knowledge

The index can include Writer pages, projects, tasks, calendar events, files, supported memories, imported conversations, and imported assistant data. Search results retain source information so responses can cite the context used.

Profiles and memory

Create assistant profiles with name, role, bio, tone, personality, custom instructions, avatar, memories, conversations, and imported source details. Existing imported metadata remains backward compatible.

Tools

Supported tools can search records and perform explicit actions such as creating tasks or finding workspace content. Tools operate within Merthyra’s own data boundaries and report when required information is missing.

Optional web research

Web research is a separate privacy setting and is not treated as automatic background browsing. When disabled, the assistant stays within the context Merthyra provides from the workspace and system model.

Availability

Apple Foundation Models require a supported device and operating-system configuration. Merthyra should explain model unavailability rather than fabricate a successful response.

Find anything

Search & command routing

Global search

Search titles, content, tags, filenames, project records, tasks, calendar events, files, OCR text, research items, and supported imported knowledge. Results route to the correct destination and workspace.

Writer search

Writer search includes title, body, section, tag, filename, and OCR attachment context. Advanced search can use case sensitivity and regular expressions. Find and replace can operate on a page or across the workspace.

Quick Open and commands

Writer includes Quick Open and a command palette for keyboard-first navigation, creation, formatting, layout changes, and page actions. App commands provide additional keyboard shortcuts on Mac and iPad keyboards.

Saved searches

Store useful Writer queries and smart filters for repeated access to recent, favorite, pinned, archived, tagged, or matching pages.

System integration

Apple ecosystem

App Intents and App Shortcuts

Merthyra includes intents for New Note, Quick Capture, New Task, New Project, New Collection, Search Workspace, Ask Lloer, Open Writer, Open Today’s Notes, Open Recent Project, Scan Document, New Calendar Event, Start Focus Session, Resume Last Workspace, and calendar queries. Ten promoted App Shortcuts are exposed within Apple’s per-app limit; other intents remain available in Shortcuts.

Action Button

On supported iPhones, assign a Merthyra App Shortcut from Apple Settings. No private Action Button API or background hack is used.

Widgets

WidgetKit includes Today’s Tasks, Focus Timer, Recent Notes, Today’s Agenda, Quick Capture, Recent Projects, Productivity Dashboard, Calendar + Tasks, Workspace Overview, and supported Lock Screen or StandBy status widgets.

Live Activities and Dynamic Island

ActivityKit presentations cover Focus Session, Task Timer, Transfer Progress, Meeting, Writing Goal, and Deadline. Tapping a supported activity deep-links back into the relevant Merthyra destination.

Spotlight, Siri, and Shortcuts

Supported entities can be discoverable through Spotlight. Natural-language phrases and parameters make core creation and navigation actions available through Siri and the Shortcuts app.

Reliability

Sync, backup & data safety

File-backed storage

Merthyra stores its core workspace documents in managed files. Individual feature stores use stable schemas, backward-compatible decoding, stable IDs, and modification dates.

iCloud conflict merging

When multiple file versions exist, stores merge by logical record identity and modification time. Writer also uses section deletion, restoration, Trash purge, and migration records to avoid resurrecting stale pages or sections.

Autosave

Editor saves are debounced. Selection changes and navigation are guarded so a deleted page cannot be recreated by a delayed autosave. Failed saves roll back in-memory destructive operations where supported.

Migration

The unified Writer migration imports legacy documents by source ID, not title. It preserves body, title, dates, workspace, folders or collections, and tags where possible. The migration is versioned and idempotent.

Manual exports

Writer and supported workspace tools provide manual export or backup paths. Keep independent copies of irreplaceable files, especially during beta testing or major operating-system updates.

Personalize

Appearance & accessibility

Themes

Merthyra includes Light, Dark, Graphite, Dracula, Aurora, and custom theme support. Liquid Glass appears in Merthyra themes while Dracula provides a crisp opaque alternative. Custom palettes and saved presets are available in Appearance settings.

Writer typography

Adjust editor font, font size, line spacing, and maximum editor width per page. OpenDyslexic is included as an accessibility-friendly font option.

Motion and transparency

Reduce Motion disables or simplifies nonessential transitions. Reduced-transparency and accessibility conditions replace glass surfaces with solid, higher-contrast panels.

VoiceOver and keyboard

Interactive controls use labels, dynamic section disclosure actions, accessible destructive actions, and keyboard navigation where supported. Writer section arrows announce Expand or Collapse with the section name.

Dynamic Type and compact layouts

iPhone sheets use compact single-column layouts, scrollable content, safe-area spacing, and touch-sized controls. Larger text settings may reduce how much content fits at once, so scroll rather than clipping is the expected behavior.

Platforms

Mac, iPhone, iPad & visionOS

Mac

Mac provides the full multi-column workspace, pointer context menus, keyboard commands, stable Writer tabs, dual documents, file drag-and-drop, native previews, and desktop window management.

iPhone

iPhone uses a five-tab bottom bar: Home, Writer, Tasks, Calendar, and More. Writer opens library first and then a full-screen page editor. Zen Mode hides the tab bar while preserving writing-goal progress.

iPad

iPad keeps the spacious sidebar experience when width permits, adapts to touch, supports keyboard workflows, document scanning, and PencilKit handwriting.

Apple Vision Pro

Build 63 includes a native visionOS destination and a spacious window configuration. UIKit compatibility, URL opening, clipboard behavior, email, themes, file-backed storage, App Groups, iCloud, and Sign in with Apple are platform-gated for visionOS. iPhone-only features such as document scanning, Pencil handwriting, Lock Screen widgets, and Dynamic Island remain hidden where unsupported.

Feature availability

Some system capabilities depend on device hardware, OS version, permissions, Apple account settings, or provider configuration. Merthyra uses availability guards rather than showing unsupported controls everywhere.

Help

FAQ & support

Where is my data?

Core workspace content is stored locally and in Merthyra’s iCloud container where iCloud is enabled. Exact files vary by feature. Use exports and your own backups for important work.

Why can’t a creator account connect?

The app must have the provider’s registered OAuth client ID, redirect configuration, and secure token-exchange endpoint. A development build without those values can open the provider website but cannot complete linking.

Why is Apple Foundation Models unavailable?

The feature depends on supported Apple hardware, OS version, language or region requirements, and Apple Intelligence configuration. Merthyra cannot bypass those system requirements.

Can deleted Writer items return?

Current Writer files include deletion, Trash, purge, and restoration records designed to suppress stale conflict copies. If an item still returns, report the exact sequence and whether iCloud was active on another device.

How do I report a bug?

Use the Bug Reports forum and include device, OS version, app build, exact steps, expected behavior, actual behavior, and screenshots where safe. You can also email support.merlin@thesmol.one.

Feature requests

Submit ideas in the Feature Requests forum. Explain the problem, who it helps, and how the feature should behave across Mac, iPhone, iPad, and visionOS.

Beta reminder: Merthyra is still prerelease software. Keep backups and avoid storing the only copy of critical or regulated information in the beta.
