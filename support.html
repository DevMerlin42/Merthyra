Good to know.

Answers based on the features in the current app.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Choose Apple Health during onboarding, or open settings from the top-right of Wellness. Turn on the individual dashboard features you want, then allow the corresponding read permissions in Apple’s sheet.

Why is a wellness measurement missing?

A feature may be off, permission may not be granted, or Apple Health may not have a readable record. Merthyra Day leaves unavailable measurements empty rather than inventing values. Check that the corresponding records exist in Apple Health.

Does the dashboard read Apple Watch information?

It can read authorized records that your Apple Watch has saved to Apple Health on your iPhone. Availability depends on the measurements recorded, the permissions you allow, and sync between your devices.

Can I change my goals or disconnect a source?

Yes. Use Wellness settings for optional personal goals and Apple Health choices. Use Calendar & Reminders in More for source selections or to disconnect. Disconnecting leaves your Apple events and reminders intact.

Which calendars and reminder lists are shown?

Connected sources can be filtered in Calendar & Reminders settings. If none are selected, all available sources are shown. Things groups the day’s connected events and reminders into progress cards.

How do water units work?

Choose progress by volume or glasses. Volume can be mL or fl oz. When you choose glasses, set the size of one glass. The same preference is shared by Home, Wellness, Water, and the Apple Watch water companion.

Does Journal sync with Apple Journal?

Merthyra Day keeps its own journal entries. You can share a separate copy to Apple Journal when the system share destination is available. Entries do not automatically sync in both directions.

Where are alarms, stopwatch, and Focus Timer?

Open Clocks in the bottom bar. It contains all three time tools. iOS controls notification and alarm permissions, and its system alarm Stop remains available.

Can I turn affirmation notifications off?

Yes. Change the setting in Wellness preferences or during onboarding. You can leave notifications off, or choose how often they arrive within your wake-up and wind-down window.

How do I delete my Merthyra iCloud data?

Open More, then iCloud & your data. Delete all Merthyra data removes your private CloudKit data and local Merthyra data on this device after you close and reopen the app. Read the confirmation carefully. Your Apple Health, Calendar, Reminders, and subscription are kept.

Which devices does the current build support?

The current app targets iPhone with iOS 27 and the optional water companion targets Apple Watch with watchOS 27. Use the requirements shown by the live app listing when a public release is available.
