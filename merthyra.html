Writer Pro

Writing tools that disappear when you start writing.

Merthyra’s Writer combines a minimal editor with deep controls that stay available when you need them not permanently in your way.

  • Four layouts. Editor, Preview, side-by-side Split, and Combined live-rendered editing.
  • Real organization. Sections, nested document types, pinned pages, favorites, archive, tabs, saved searches, and Trash.
  • Writing intelligence. Readability, grade level, passive voice, weak verbs, adverbs, filler, clichés, repetition, paragraph density, and vocabulary diversity.
  • Long-form focus. Books, chapters, scenes, word goals, writing sessions, Zen Mode, Typewriter Mode, version history, and session restore.

Merthyra Intelligence

Your workspace can answer back.

Ask questions across Writer pages, tasks, calendar events, projects, files, memories, and imported context. Merthyra retrieves the relevant workspace information and uses Apple Foundation Models on supported devices.

  • Optional web research only when you explicitly enable it.

Designed around your data

Real files. Clear boundaries. No fake cloud.

Merthyra’s core workspace is file-backed and scoped by workspace. It works offline, syncs through your Apple account where enabled, and keeps conflict handling tied to stable IDs and modification dates.

Read the privacy policy
Workspace-scopedWriter pages, tasks, projects, files, spreadsheets, and settings stay attached to the correct workspace.
Offline-capableThe core app remains useful without a permanent connection. Changes save locally and synchronize when services are available.
Conflict-awareStable identifiers, modification dates, deletion records, and migration manifests protect against duplicates and stale copies.
Permission-basedCalendar, location, notifications, creator accounts, scanning, and other integrations activate only when you choose them.
