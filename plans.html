A few helpful details.

Where can I see the current price?

Open the Pro screen in Merthyra Day. Available monthly and annual prices come directly from Apple’s StoreKit offers for your region.

Do I need to connect Apple Health?

No. Apple Health is optional. Turn on only the features you want to read, and use Merthyra Day’s other tools without connecting it.

Can I restore my subscription?

Yes. Use Restore Purchase on the app’s Pro screen while signed in to the Apple account used for your subscription.
