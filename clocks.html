MAKE TIME FOR WHAT MATTERS

Your time.
In one place.

Open the Clocks tab for Focus Timer, alarms, and a stopwatch. Choose the tool that fits the moment.

  • Focus TimerSet a session, link what you’re working on, and take a break.
  • AlarmsSet a morning alarm using Apple’s default alarm sound.
  • A wake-up challengeStart your morning with simple math, water, and movement prompts.
  • StopwatchTrack elapsed time and save laps while it runs.

iOS controls alarm permissions and dismissal. System Stop remains available.
