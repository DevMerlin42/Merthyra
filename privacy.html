YOUR DAY STAYS YOURS

Personal by design.

Your wellbeing deserves a little privacy.

Merthyra Day uses optional connections to bring your information together. Here is how the current app handles it.

Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Merthyra Day requests only the dashboard features you turn on: steps, sleep, heart rate, active calories, mindfulness, and exercise.

Measurements are processed on your iPhone. They stay out of Merthyra’s iCloud sync, widgets, Watch transfers, advertising, and analytics. Turning a feature off clears it from the dashboard and stops its queries.

Your schedule and tasks

Merthyra Day reads your chosen Apple calendars and reminder lists. Edits save directly to Apple Calendar and Reminders. Task estimates, selected priorities, and related Focus history are Merthyra metadata that can sync privately through iCloud.

You can select or disconnect sources in Calendar & Reminders settings. Disconnecting stops Merthyra Day from reading or editing that source on your device; your Apple events and reminders are kept. Apple permissions can also be revoked in iOS Settings.

Your reflections and preferences

Your profile, personal preferences, journal entries, and completed Focus history can sync through your private iCloud database. Merthyra Day does not require a separate Merthyra account and has no advertising, tracking, or cloud AI.

Permission choices, active timers, stopwatch state, notification schedules, and system alarms remain specific to each device. You can manage iCloud sync in More → iCloud & your data. Delete all Merthyra data removes the app’s local information on this device and its private CloudKit data after you close and reopen the app; it keeps your Apple records and subscription.

Water and Apple Watch

Merthyra Day keeps its own water log and doesn’t write water records to Apple Health. The Apple Watch companion saves water entries locally and syncs with the paired iPhone when connected. It does not receive the health dashboard’s measurements.

Notifications and alarms

Optional local notifications use your daily rhythm and selected preferences. iOS controls permission, delivery, and alarm dismissal. Summaries can become outdated while the app is closed.

This website

This static website has no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or contact forms. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement.

The screenshots are captured from Merthyra Day using example data. They do not display a customer’s personal calendar or health records.
