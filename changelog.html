Website Redesign

Merthyra.com was redesigned as the home of the growing Merthyra app family.

  • Introduced a new app hub homepage for discovering Merthyra products.
  • Added dedicated landing pages for Merthyra and Merthyra Control.
  • Created clearer navigation between apps, documentation, community pages, and product updates.
  • Redesigned the Merthyra Control page with colorful liquid glass visuals, playful motion, and a fun Mac inspired style.
  • Improved responsive layouts across Mac, iPhone, and iPad screen sizes.
