APPLE, BUILT IN

It belongs on your Apple devices.

Merthyra is built as a native workspace instead of a browser shell. Calendar and Reminders can connect to Apple services, Sign in with Apple handles the account layer, and iCloud Drive keeps portable workspace files close to the system you already use.

YOUR DATA

A workspace should not become a trap.

Merthyra keeps personal work local-first and mirrors workspace content into practical file formats in iCloud Drive. The goal is simple: the work should still make sense outside the app that created it.
